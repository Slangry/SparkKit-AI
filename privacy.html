Privacy notice

Draft notice — review before public launch.

This early version of SparkKit runs its draft generators in your browser. The current version does not send your entered text to a SparkKit server, create an account, or store drafts in a SparkKit database. Your browser, device, extensions and hosting provider may process technical information as part of normal website operation.

Do not enter passwords, financial details, identity numbers, confidential business information or other sensitive personal data into the tools.

Future versions may add an AI service, analytics, advertising, accounts or payments. Those features must not be activated until this notice is updated to explain what data is collected, why it is processed, which service providers receive it, retention periods and how users can exercise applicable privacy rights.

Contact: replace this line with a monitored business email before launch.
